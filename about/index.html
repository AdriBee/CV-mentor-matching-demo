HOW IT WORKS

From shared interests
to a first conversation.

Use this directory to explore possible mentors and prepare a thoughtful introduction.

01

Explore your interests

Search a research topic, specialty, method, or name. Use research areas and filters to narrow the directory. Multiple search words refine your results.

02

Build a shortlist

Open profiles to explore projects, research methods, and desired skills. Save mentors to return to them later in this browser.

03

Start a conversation

Introduce yourself, explain what interests you about the research, and share your experience and availability. A listed opportunity is a starting point for discussion.

About the profiles

Profiles are maintained in a reviewed JSON collection in the repository. Only approved profiles appear here. Mentoring availability and project details are confirmed separately.

Shortlists stay in your browser. This site does not create a student account, send messages, or track mentorship requests.
